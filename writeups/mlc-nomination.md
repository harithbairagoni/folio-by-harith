# Preparing a nomination portfolio

## Problem

A varied record of public-service and professional work needed to be organized into a coherent portfolio for formal nomination proceedings.

## My contribution

I prepared the portfolio submitted for the nomination process. The subject's achievements belong to the subject; my work was preparing and organizing the document.

## The work

- Organize profile material into thematic sections.
- Structure public-service, policy, professional-experience, and education material.
- Make the document navigable with a contents structure and section hierarchy.
- Bring profile information and supporting material into one review document.

## Deliverable

A structured nomination portfolio. Submission is the established deliverable; approval is not claimed as an outcome of my work.

## Evidence boundary

This is a recreated process summary. It omits the subject's identity, photographs, family and personal information, identifying biographical details, quotations, affiliations, and original pages. No private contact list, voter list, political targeting data, or internal campaign organization is included. No new numerical outcome is inferred.
