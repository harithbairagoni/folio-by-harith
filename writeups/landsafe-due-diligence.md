# LandSafe: from records to a decision-ready report

## Problem

Property review requires documentary records and physical observations to be considered together. A report needs to make those different checks understandable without hiding the unresolved questions.

## My contribution

As Director of Operations & Co-Founder, I coordinated verification workflows across field operations, GIS, and legal teams. The supplied work sample shows how that work is assembled into a structured property report. Specialist legal and survey conclusions are team inputs, not claims that I personally provided legal advice or conducted every survey.

## The work

- Organize title flow and supporting transaction records.
- Bring encumbrance history and release documentation alongside revenue records.
- Distinguish legal and regulatory checks from physical verification.
- Structure observations, supporting evidence, and next actions in one report.
- Use a supporting-document index so the report remains connected to its evidence.

## Deliverables evidenced by the supplied material

A report structure covering title flow, encumbrances, compliance, physical verification, market context, a category-based review matrix, recommendations, and supporting records. Supporting SOPs describe document sourcing, assigned responsibilities, missing-record follow-up, escalation, review, and delivery. A separate SOP describes monitoring; it is a documented workflow, not evidence that every proposed monitoring capability was deployed.

## Evidence boundary

This is an anonymized method summary, not a redacted original report or legal opinion. It does not reproduce a client's property, findings, transaction terms, or recommendation. The previously supplied résumé metrics describe broader work and are not attributed to this individual case. No new outcome figure is inferred from these files.

Names, family relationships, addresses, locations, coordinates, survey and registration numbers, dates tied to transactions, measurements, valuations, mortgage details, scores, signatures, QR codes, photographs, and document links are withheld.
