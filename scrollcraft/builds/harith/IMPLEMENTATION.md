## Creative system

The explicitly delegated direction is an evidence-led portfolio, with a photographic amber opening, paper product demonstrations, a forest coordination study, and a dark contact ending. Persistent section navigation allows visitors to skip ahead. This custom grammar was selected because none of the eight stock grammars preserve both the requested personal introduction and skippable work demonstrations without changing their constraints. The detailed comparisons, journey, feeling curve, peak and device score are in `BRIEF.md`.

The signature is a coordination lens: foundations, mechanical equipment, and documents start as separate assemblies on desktop and converge as the visitor moves through Experience. Selecting a discipline highlights its model layer and changes a nearby explanation. Phones start with the assembled model in normal document flow. Motion-sensitive visitors get the complete assembled scene without extra pinned travel.

The fingerprint registry was empty before planning. There were no earlier rows to collide with.
